# Review v2: content, facts, independence (2026-11-12)

Reviewer: independent, read-only. Clone of `/home/claude/primer` main @ 8ebfc8a in `/tmp/review-content`. Built with `cd app && npm ci && npm run build` (167 KB, 11 files). Rendered all five routes in headless Chrome and dumped text, aria-labels, alt/title attributes and link targets. `validateApp(app, {strict:true})` → `[]`. Card indices are 0-based positions in `cards[]`.

## Verdict

The history and economics hold up. Every dated claim in What Lasts, Vote With Your Feet and the Elias lesson checks out against primary or standard sources, and the six source entries resolve. The weak lesson is **Own Your Exit**. It is the old contest chapter with the names taken out. Its middle cards describe one unnamed protocol (a registry number, app keys, yearly storage rent) as if that were how anyone owns their exit. Its story card retells the contest's entries and vouch rule as if they happened on their own. Its only source (Hirschman) partly argues against its thesis. On credits, 3 of the 4 builders in that lesson show as "by a creator" even though their usernames can be looked up. No banned word reaches the UI, but three indirect leaks get past the regex: the commissioner's slogan "friend speech", the brief's "three commands and a witness" and "practical freedom of association", and the validator's word list, which ships in the JS bundle. The inline videos have not been checked for spoken or on-screen platform references. Nothing is a blocker, but L1 needs a rewrite before the app can honestly be called independent.

Counts: **blocker 0 · major 7 · minor 10 · nit 9**

## Findings

### Major

**C1 · credits: 3 of 4 Own Your Exit builders render "by a creator"**
- Where: `content/build/app.json` creators `2391`, `268438`, `381353` (lines ~520–540) have no `username`. `content/seed/people.json` lacks these fids.
- Evidence: rendered "by a creator·original ↗" ×3 on L1's "more" card. The public hub `userDataByFid` gives 2391 → `ramitphi` (Phunk, 2nd), 268438 → `cashlessman.eth` (Kite), 381353 → `aap` (runfcx). All three pass the contract's username regex.
- Fix: add the three to `content/seed/people.json`, or run the online refresh (`refresh.mjs` step 5 resolves usernames) and recommit `app.json`. Add a compile check that fails when a lead/more work's creator has no username.
- Lane: ingest (refresh/build), planner (seed).

**C2 · L1 sells one unnamed protocol's design as the general way to own your exit**
- Where: `own-your-exit.json:32,36,40` (cards[5–7]).
- Evidence: "An account number in a public registry, owned by a key you hold"; "You rent the storage yearly." That is Farcaster's FID/IdRegistry, signer and storage-unit model (chapter `exit.json` essay). It is true of no network the reader can identify.
- Why it matters: the reader can't act on these cards or check them. Two lines later the practice card only says "export into a folder". The promise ("how to own what you'd take with you") is delivered for a system the lesson won't name.
- Fix: open with "Some open networks split an account into parts:" and keep the parts generic: a name you control (e.g. your own domain as your handle), keys you can revoke, signed posts any app can show. Drop "rent the storage yearly", or label it as one network's cost.
- Lane: lessons-a.

**C3 · L1 story retells the contest (indirect leak, misleading, unsourced)**
- Where: `own-your-exit.json:49–50` (cards[9]).
- What's wrong:
  - The heading "Three commands and a witness" is the brief's spec ("three commands… a public vouch", chapter `exit.json` essay[0–1]). The card text never mentions three commands.
  - "about two dozen guides appeared" in one October week only happened because there was a prize.
  - "The convincing ones came with a witness" presents a required vouch as a mark of quality.
  - "proposed ten changes" is uncredited and has drifted: the builder's page now says "13 fixes sent to Farcaster's own repos" (https://sats100m.github.io/farcaster-in-3/).
- Fix: replace it with a story that isn't the contest, or a generic one ("A how-to is only proven when a stranger finishes it…"). Drop the date, the count and "ten".
- Lane: lessons-a.

**C4 · L2 practice uses the commissioner's slogan, undefined**

- Evidence: "Practice friend speech this week, not foe speech". The lesson never defines either term. "Friend speech" is the new owner's call in the prize post (chapter `friend-speech.json` "minute"). "Friend speech over foe speech" is also an uncredited entry's framing (seed `0xeee4d8e1` gloss). The slug also shows in the address bar as `#/l/friend-speech`.
- Fix: "This week, disagree once with someone you don't know well, without making an enemy." Planner/app: consider renaming the id (it is part of the route).
- Lane: lessons-a; planner/app for the id.

**C5 · L1's only source cuts against its thesis, and its specific claims are unsourceable**
- Where: `own-your-exit.json:16` (cards[1] "House rules stay fair when leaving is cheap") and `:83–89` (sources).
- What's wrong: Hirschman holds that "the greater the availability of exit, the less likely voice will be used". For some goods "a tight monopoly could be preferable" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exit,_Voice,_and_Loyalty). L3 uses him for exactly that objection, but L1 cites him in support and never mentions it.
- The lesson's concrete facts (2 TB, October 2026 guides, storage rent, keys) come from Farcaster/Snapchain docs, which `validateSource` forbids as URLs. They are true (2 TB: https://snapchain.farcaster.xyz/getting-started), but the reader gets no source.
- Fix: one line in cards[1–3] or the counter: "Hirschman's warning: when leaving is too easy, people stop speaking up." Either cut the platform-specific numbers or have the planner allow a URL-less source note for them.
- Lane: lessons-a, planner.

**C6 · The arc contradicts itself on online exit**
- Where: `own-your-exit.json:20` (cards[2]) "Leaving means starting from zero" vs `vote-with-your-feet.json:38` (cards[7]) "Online comes closer to his model: switching takes a minute".
- Why: the chapter qualified this ("your name and your follows are not locked inside any one app"). The lesson dropped the qualifier, so a reader going 1→3 gets opposite claims.
- Fix (L3): "Online, switching can take a minute, if your name and connections come with you."
- Lane: lessons-b.
