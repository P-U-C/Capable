# capable v3: user testing

Build tested: `main` at d391dbc, cloned to `/tmp/usertest`. `app.json` was compiled offline first, as `deploy.yml` does, so the live stamps contract `0xdDe6…C692` is configured (see T18). The build was served under `/Capable/` on 127.0.0.1. Personas 2 and 3 also ran on `https://p-u-c.github.io/Capable/`, answered locally from the same `dist` bytes. That gives share links an https origin, as on Pages, and puts the real Pages host in the sign-in domain.

Screenshots: `/home/claude/reviews/usertest-shots/` (121 files, named `pN-NN-…`). Harness, probes and accessibility-tree dumps: `/home/claude/reviews/usertest-tools/`. Nothing in the repository was changed. `/home/claude/primer` has since moved to 67aeba2, which is not tested here.

## Verdict

capable works for all six personas. The reading is good, the passport and stamps feel earned, and the wallet and sign-in flows handle refusals well: "Nothing was saved" and "Nothing was sent" come up exactly when they should. The Mini App path signed in silently in about 0.2 s, minted with the host wallet in one tap plus a confirm, and shared through the host's compose sheet.

There are no blockers. Two interaction defects in the deck hurt every phone reader, though:

- **T1, swiping is broken on touch.** A swipe that starts on a card's body never moves the deck in Chromium touch emulation, so "swipe to begin" does nothing. Only taps work.
- **T2, a tap "clicks through".** The tap that advances also clicks whatever lands under the finger on the next card. Tapping through "read more" skips the "up next" card and drops the reader into the next lesson's cover. Tapping through "sit with it" ticks a practice step the reader never did.

Both have one-line fixes, and the T1 fix is verified below.

The share loop also has a hole (T3). The link a reader shares opens on "A CITIZEN 0x7a11…ffee", with no sentence saying what capable is. Its only call to action sits about two screens down.

The independence rules hold in the app: only the six allow-listed identity strings name Farcaster, and none of the banned words appear. The README breaks the strict rule in three lines (T12). The copy around stamps still assumes a crypto reader (T11 and the copy pass).

## Findings

Severity: blocker, major, minor, nit. Owner: app (code/copy in `app/`), content (lessons), planner (README, data, spec).

### Major

**T1: Swipes that start on a card's body don't move the deck on touch.**
- Personas: 1, 2, 4 (any phone).
- Screen: lesson cover ("swipe to begin") and every card.
- Screenshots: `p1-09-l1-swipe-did-nothing.png`, `p4-03-l1-swipe-did-nothing.png`.
- What happened: a left swipe from the middle of the cover snapped back.
  - Pointer trace: `pointerdown → 1× pointermove → pointercancel`.
  - Started on the card head (the "// idea" row), the same swipe works.
  - Started on the body, it fails on every card tested (cover, idea; four swipes each run).
  - Root cause: `.card__body` is `overflow-y: auto`, which makes it a scroll container. Touch-action stops at the nearest scroll container, so the stage's `touch-action: pan-y` doesn't apply inside the body. The browser claims the horizontal pan and cancels the pointer, and `gesture.mjs` treats the cancel as "snap back".
  - Isolated in a 10-line page (`usertest-tools/touchprobe2.py`): a nested `overflow-y:auto` child cancels; giving that child `touch-action: pan-y` makes 8/8 moves arrive.
- Why it matters: swipe is the advertised gesture, and the first thing a reader tries fails. Taps and keys still work, which is why this isn't a blocker. It affects Chromium on Android and the Android host's WebView; iOS WebKit was not tested.
- Fix: `.card__body { touch-action: pan-y; }`, plus any other inner scroller in a card. Verified: with it set, 4/4 swipes from the body advance (`usertest-tools/swipefix.py`), and vertical scrolling inside long cards still works.
- Owner: app.

**T2: The tap that advances also clicks what lands under the finger on the next card.**
- Personas: 1, 2, 4.
- Screens: "read more", then "up next"; "sit with it", then "do this".
- Screenshots: `p1-24-l1-15-deeper.png` → `p1-25-l1-landed-elsewhere.png`, `p2-13-…`, `p4-07-…`, `p4-12-…`; practice: `p1-21-l1-12-practice.png`, `p2-07-l1-12-practice.png`.
- What happened: `gesture.mjs` advances on `pointerup`. The browser then fires the tap's `click` at the same point, about 1 ms later, and it hits the new card. Scan of every card in lessons 1 and 2, tapping the right third at mid-height (`usertest-tools/ghostscan.py`):
  - `deeper → next`: the route jumps to the next lesson. The "up next" card, "N lessons left" and "All lessons" are never seen.
  - `question → practice`: step 2 is ticked without the reader touching it.
  - On the practice card itself, the right-third tap lands on a step (they span the card's width) and toggles it instead of advancing, so the reader's "next" unticks step 2.
  - The other 26 of the 32 transitions were clean. `suppressClick` is set only for drags and holds, not taps.
- Why it matters: it hides the lesson's ending and the "what's next" hand-off, the very thing persona 1 was looking for. It also silently records practice the reader didn't do.
- Fix: after a tap-advance, swallow the next click on the stage for about 400 ms (by timestamp; a `setTimeout(0)` flag can expire before the click arrives). Alternatively, advance on `click` for taps instead of on `pointerup`. On the practice card, leave a narrow tap gutter on the right edge, or accept that taps there tick steps and say "swipe to go on". Add a test that taps through `deeper` and asserts the route.
- Owner: app.

**T3: A shared passport link is a dead end for someone new.**
- Personas: 1 (and 2's audience).
- Screen: `#/p/<address>`.
- Screenshots: `p1-01-arrive-shared-passport.png`, `p1-02-shared-passport-bottom.png`, `p2-17-public-passport-from-cast.png`.
- What happened:
  - The first screen says "A CITIZEN", shows a hex address, stamp tiles marked "on chain" / "not yet", and the citizen line.
  - Nothing says these are short lessons, how long they take, or that they're free.
  - "Start your own" is the only CTA, about two screens down at 390×844.
- Why it matters: Share's whole job is to bring people here. The persona needed two scrolls and a guess.
- Fix: put a compact strip above the fold: "capable: 4 three-minute lessons on starting a community that lasts. Free, no account." plus "Start lesson 01 →". Label the address ("wallet 0x7a11…ffee") and say what the stamps mean ("earned by finishing a lesson").
- Owner: app (copy: planner).

### Minor

**T4: No way to reach or copy your own public passport.**
- Personas: 3, 4.
- Screen: passport. Screenshots: `p3-16`, `p3-17-passport-after-mint-bottom.png`.
- What happened: the passport has no link to `#/p/<address>`, shows no address, and has no "copy link". The only route is the embed inside the Farcaster compose URL. Signed-out readers can only share through Farcaster.
- Why it matters: the task "view your public passport" needed URL surgery, and readers without Farcaster can't share at all.
- Fix: add "View public passport ↗" once an address is known, plus "Copy link" (or `navigator.share`) next to the Farcaster share.
- Owner: app.

**T5: Sign out leaves the wallet address and the mint record in the browser.**
- Persona: 3. Screen: passport after sign-out. Screenshot: `p3-20-after-sign-out.png`.
- What happened: `capable.citizen@/Capable/` keeps `wallet` and `minted.sovereign` after sign-out. The signed-out passport still reads the chain for that wallet and shows "on chain".
- Why it matters: on a shared computer, the next person sees, and shares, someone else's stamps.
- Fix: clear `wallet` and `minted` on sign-out, or add "Forget this browser". Say which one happens.
- Owner: app.

**T6: The app never says it's free and needs no account, or who made it and why.**
- Personas: 4, 1. Screens: home, passport. Screenshot: `p4-16-home-bottom-credits.png`.
- What happened: the README's "free · no account needed · works offline" and its principles (no tracking, no server) appear nowhere in the app. The only maker line is "Independent." No external links exist on home.
- Why it matters: the wary reader can verify it only with devtools. "Independent" without a name raises suspicion instead of lowering it.
- Fix: one line under the home facts ("Free. No account. Nothing leaves this browser unless you mint.") and an about line with a source link (for example "Open source ↗" to the repo). This needs no commissioner or contest wording.
- Owner: app + planner.

**T7: Blank screen for 7.4 s on slow 3G.**

## What could not be tested, and how to check it on a real phone in 5 minutes

**Not tested here (sandbox):**
- No real Farcaster client. The Mini App SDK, its context, `ready()`, the host wallet and `composeCast` were stubs.
- No real relay, Optimism or Base RPC. All were mocked. The SIWF signature check itself ran for real in `auth-client`.
- No real wallet, extension popups or gas.
- No real video streams (a local test stream stood in).
- No iOS Safari / WebKit.
- No real phone touch hardware. T1 and T2 come from Chromium's touch emulation, and the mechanism matches Blink's real behavior, but they still need a device check.
- No service worker / offline / install (service workers were blocked).
- No real-world network (throttling is emulated).
- None of the sandbox's network failures are counted as defects.

**5-minute phone check:**
1. On an Android phone (Chrome) and an iPhone (Safari), open `https://p-u-c.github.io/Capable/` and tap **Start lesson 01**. On the cover, put your thumb in the middle of the card and **swipe left**. If it springs back, T1 is real on that browser. Then swipe starting on the "// lesson 01" label row; that one should work.
2. Tap the right edge to reach "sit with it", then tap the right edge once more. If step 2 on "do this" is already ticked, T2 is real.
3. Keep tapping the right edge through "read more". If you land on lesson 02's cover without seeing "up next", T2 is confirmed.
4. In the Farcaster app, cast the link `https://p-u-c.github.io/Capable/`, open it from the cast and check the chip shows your picture. Finish lesson 01, tap **Mint this stamp**, confirm in the wallet sheet (a few cents on Base), then tap **Share on Farcaster** and open the embed it makes.
5. Open that shared link in a private browser tab, as a stranger would, and see whether you know what capable is before scrolling (T3).
6. Optional: in Chrome devtools, set Slow 3G and 4× CPU and reload with the cache disabled. Count the seconds of blank screen (T7).

## Method notes

- Each persona is a scripted Playwright session: `usertest-tools/usertest.py` plus `usertest_personas.py`, which extend `app/tools/shoot.py`'s mocks.
- Reading time is estimated at 200 wpm from each card's word count.
- Taps use Playwright's touchscreen; swipes use CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`.
- Logs are JSON per persona, and the accessibility trees are saved as YAML in `usertest-tools/`.
- Persona 2's taps ran at device scale 1 because of a Playwright cross-origin-iframe touch scaling artifact (taps landed at half the coordinates at scale 2). That is not an app defect.
- In persona 6, the "updated" scenario uses a fabricated draft lesson ("Rules People \*Keep\*"). The literal asterisks in `p6-07` are from that test data, not a defect.
