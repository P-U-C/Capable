# capable v2: UX, visual and accessibility review

Reviewer: independent (did not build it). Build: fresh clone of `/home/claude/primer` @ `8ebfc8a`, `app/` `npm ci && npm run build` (11 files, 167 KB). Walkthrough in headless Chrome at 390×844, 320×568 and 1280×800, plus 640×400 / 320×200 (desktop 200% / 400% zoom), 844×390 landscape, and reduced motion. Scripts were kept outside the repo (`/tmp/ux-tools/`); they reuse `app/tools/shoot.py`'s local server and its stubs. Screenshots: `/home/claude/reviews/ux-shots/` (paths below are relative to it).

**Sandbox note.** Google Fonts, cdnjs (hls.js) and `stream.farcaster.xyz` can't be reached from here. Fonts and hls.js are served from local packages, and video is an ffmpeg test pattern. The colour bars in the video shots come from that stub and are not a defect. `m01-nofonts-home.png` and `m02-nofonts-cover.png` show the Arial fallback that appears when no stub is used. That is also not counted as a defect.

## Verdict

The visual language is a real success. Chunky outlined objects, hard shadows, candy colours on warm off-white, an outlined key word in the Shout titles, pixel eyebrows, mono credits, flat colour chips and screenprint scenes all read clearly as Beep's language without copying Beep's brand. The deck engine is solid: tap thirds, swipe, arrows, Space, Home/End and Esc all work. Position, completion and ticks survive a reload, inactive cards are inert, and reduced motion really does crossfade. The weak point is the experience around the content.

- **First run:** a newcomer isn't told what this is (communities) or what to do. The only call to action sits on the fold.
- **Return visits:** home never points to the next lesson. A finished lesson keeps a loud magenta "READ AGAIN".
- **The emotional peak:** the trait card is the quietest card in the deck.
- **Wrong message:** the last lesson says "ALL CAUGHT UP" while two lessons are still unread.
- **Back button:** Back re-opens the lesson you just closed.
- **Video:** it can't be paused from the keyboard, there's no scrub bar, and there are no captions.
- **Zoom:** above 200% the fixed-height card turns into a small scrolling window, and the "more below" button covers text.

Nothing blocks use. Eight majors stand between this and "delightful".

**Counts:** 0 blocker · 8 major · 9 minor · 6 nit.

## Findings

### Major

**U1. First run doesn't say what this is, why it matters, or where to start.** Screen: home. Shots: `n01-firstrun-390.png`, `n02b-firstrun-390-full-small.png`, `b-40-home-320.png`.
- **What's wrong:** above the fold you see the logo "capable", the line "BECOME CAPABLE." (which repeats the logo), "one idea at a time", a door picture, and a "// you | 0 of 4" sticker that means nothing yet. The definition ("Able to start a community that lasts…") appears only in the "you" panel, about 3,000px down. There is no onboarding. At 390 the first START button sits at y=800–848 of an 844px screen, cut by the fold; at 320 it is about two screens down. The page is 3,651px tall for four lessons.
- **Why it matters:** the "why care" is invisible, so a first-time visitor has to guess whether this is a course, a game or a brand page.
- **Fix:**
  - Under the Shout title, show the citizen line in Body: "Learn to start a community that lasts, and to be a good member of one."
  - Add a Mono line "4 lessons · 3 min each · earn 4 traits".
  - Add one primary button, "Start lesson 01 →", that later becomes "Continue: Own Your Exit".
  - Make tile art 16:10 (it is 4:3.3 now) so the first tile's title and button show at 390.
  - Change the sticker to "0/4 traits".
- **Lane:** app (`home.mjs`, `copy.mjs`, `app.css`).

**U2. Home never points to the next lesson.** Screen: home after finishing a lesson. Shots: `n12-home-top-after-1-done.png`, `n10-home-after-1-done.png`, `d01b-desktop-home-full-small.png`.
- **What's wrong:** all four tiles carry an identical magenta primary button. A finished lesson keeps a magenta "READ AGAIN" at the top, and its "done" state is a 13px mono word. The next unread lesson is a full screen below and looks the same as the rest.
- **Why it matters:** returning users must work out where they were, and the hierarchy rewards re-reading over progressing.
- **Fix:**
  - Use magenta only on the first unfinished lesson.
  - Shrink finished tiles: a smaller art strip, a white secondary "Read again" button, and a trait-colour "done" sticker or stamp.
  - Optionally add a sticky "Up next" bar under the header.
- **Lane:** app.

**U3. The last lesson says "ALL CAUGHT UP" while lessons are still unread, and the hand-off ignores what's done.** Screen: the "next" card in `what-lasts`. Shot: `m05-lastlesson-allcaughtup-while-unfinished.png` (lessons 2 and 3 unread at the time).
- **What's wrong:** `deckModel` sets `next` to position+1, so the last lesson always shows `endShout`, and earlier lessons always point to n+1 even when it's finished.
- **Why it matters:** the copy is false, and it hides the unread lessons at exactly the moment a reader has momentum.
- **Fix:**
  - Set `next` to the first unfinished ready lesson after this one, wrapping round to the start.
  - Show "All caught up" only when every ready lesson is done.
  - Otherwise use copy like "2 left · Manners Make Strangers Safe →".
- **Lane:** app (`model.mjs`, `cards.mjs`).

**U4. Back re-opens the lesson you just closed.** Screen: deck, then Close or Esc, then home. Shot: `n04-back-after-esc.png`.
- **What's wrong:** Close (`href="#/"`) and Esc (`navigate("#/")`) both push a new history entry. Measured: home → lesson → Esc → Back lands on `#/l/own-your-exit`, and only a second Back returns home.
- **Why it matters:** in the installed PWA on Android, the system Back gesture cycles through closed lessons instead of leaving the app. Back from inside a deck works (home, with focus restored to the tile), and Forward resumes on the same card; only closing is broken.
- **Fix:** record `history.state = {from: "home"}` when a tile opens a deck. When closing, call `history.back()` if that state is present, otherwise `replaceState("#/")`.
- **Lane:** app (`main.mjs`, `view-deck.mjs`).

**U5. Video can't be paused from the keyboard, can't be scrubbed, and has no captions.** Screens: the "watch" card and the "more" sheet. Shots: `b-17-friend-speech-lead.png`, `d04-desktop-sheet.png`.
- **What's wrong:**
  - The video autoplays (muted). Pausing is only possible by tapping the middle third or press-holding.
  - The play button is hidden while the video plays, and the `<video>` element is missing from the accessibility tree.
  - Tab order is: sound pill → "original" link → prev/next card. There is no pause control.
  - Space advances to the next card instead of pausing.
  - There is no scrub bar or time display (the only position cue is the progress segment), no fullscreen, no `<track>`, and no transcript.
- **Why it matters:** fails WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide), 2.1.1 (Keyboard) and 1.2.2 (Captions). Even for mouse and touch users, a 1–3 minute explainer without seeking is frustrating.
- **Fix:**
  - Add a visible 44px play/pause pill beside the sound pill.
  - On a video card, make Space and K toggle pause.
  - Add a thin scrub bar under the frame, driven by the existing `onTime`.
  - Add `<track kind="captions">` when the source has VTT, plus a "transcript ↗" link.
  - Ingest should collect caption files where creators provide them.
- **Lane:** app (`player.mjs`, `work.mjs`) and content/ingest (captions).

**U6. The trait you earn is the quietest card in the deck.** Screen: the trait card. Shots: `n06-trait-earned.png`, `b-44-trait-320.png`, `z02-trait-logo-zoom.png`.
- **What's wrong:**
  - The card is a white sheet with "SOVEREIGN." at about 30px, smaller than the 46px cover title (Shout size depends on word length).
  - The confetti uses the pale `--confetti` tints (about 1.3:1 against the paper), so the "jiggle" is barely visible.
  - The logo fills its first letters in fixed cyan, then magenta, whatever trait was earned. Earning *Enduring* (yellow) shows a cyan "c" and a magenta "a".
  - "// 1 of 4 lessons done" is drawn as a shadowed pill that looks like a button.
  - There is no trait chip, although DESIGN-APP.md calls for one.
- **Why it matters:** this is the reward moment, and it reads as a footer.
- **Fix:**
  - Use the full trait-colour "fill" look with the trait name at `--shout-max`.
  - Add a large trait-chip sticker that settles from scale 1.15 to 1 in 200ms (fade only under reduced motion).
  - Draw confetti in the trait colour plus ink.
  - Fill logo letters in the colours of the traits actually earned.
  - Show the count as plain Pixel text.
- **Lane:** app (`cards.mjs`, `art.mjs`, `app.css`).

**U7. Most build credits read "by a creator".** Screen: the "more" card of lesson 1. Shot: `b-18-own-your-exit-more.png`.
- **What's wrong:** 3 of the 4 works in `own-your-exit` (fids 2391, 268438, 381353) have no username in `creators`, so the credit falls back to "by a creator".
- **Why it matters:** the spec promises creators "credited by name". Anonymous credit undercuts the app's respect-for-makers stance.
- **Fix:**
  - Ingest resolves usernames at compile time, and the validator rejects a work whose creator lacks one.
  - As an app fallback, credit the build's domain ("by docs.phunk.social") rather than "a creator".
- **Lane:** content/ingest (primary), app (fallback).

**U8. At 200%+ zoom, cards become small scrolling windows, and the "more below" button covers text.** Screens: deck at desktop zoom and at 320. Shots: `z-zoom200-desktop-counter.png`, `z-zoom400-desktop-idea.png`, `z-zoom200-phone-counter.png`, `b-43-counter-320.png`, `b-18-own-your-exit-more.png`, `z-landscape-phone-cover.png`.
- **What's wrong:**
  - The deck is a fixed-height frame. At 640×400 the card body is 210px tall and 16 of 17 cards scroll internally; at 320×200 the body is 68px, about two lines.
  - The 36px chevron (`.card__more`) is absolutely positioned over the body. It covers words on counter and practice cards at 320, on every zoomed card, and on lesson 1's "OPEN THE GUIDE" button.
  - In landscape the cover's title needs scrolling.
  - At phone 200% the progress segments overflow off the right edge.
- **Why it matters:** low-vision users who zoom get a keyhole view of the text. The brief says decoration must never sit over text.
- **Fix:**
  - When `max-height: 560px`, drop the stacked frame: cards switch to normal page flow with page scroll, and the progress bar becomes sticky.
  - Move the cue out of the text: centre it on the card's bottom border as a tab, and give the body bottom padding equal to the cue's height.
  - Let `.segs` wrap or switch to a single bar below 260px.
- **Lane:** app (`app.css`, `fit.mjs`).

### Minor

**U9. Text-only decks go monotonous after the first three ideas.** Shots: `s-deck-sheet-what-lasts.png`, `s-deck-sheet-vote-with-your-feet.png`, `s-deck-sheet-friend-speech.png`.
- **What's wrong:** only idea cards rotate looks. In `what-lasts`, cards 5–13 (story, story, term, counter, question, practice, trait, deeper, next) are all cream paper with ink text. Every lesson also opens with the same fill → paper → ink pattern.
- **Fix:** give each card type a fixed look:
  - counter on ink, with "but"/"reply" tabs in the trait colour;
  - question on the trait-colour fill;
  - term on ink with the big chip;
  - story with a slate pull-quote or a small strip of the scene;
  - practice with a trait-colour header band.

  Also seed the idea rotation by lesson id.
- **Lane:** app.

**U10. Several touch targets are under 44px.** Shots: `b-1a-own-your-exit-deeper.png`, `b-17-friend-speech-lead.png`.
- **What's wrong:** the "original ↗" credit links are 79×18, the "read ↗" source links are 48×18, and the sound pill is 149×36. Measured on every card of three lessons; home, the close button and the practice steps are fine.
- **Fix:** give the links inline-flex with `min-height: 44px` and vertical padding, or make each whole source item a link; give the sound pill `min-height: 44px`.
- **Lane:** app.

**U11. Screen readers get noisy names and double announcements.** No screenshot; this comes from the accessibility tree dump.
- **What's wrong:**
  - Each tile link's name starts with the scene's alt text, then "slash slash zero one": "A door ajar in a wall… // 01 Own Your Exit. … Not started".
  - Every eyebrow is read with its slashes ("// idea"), and the you-panel heading is named "// you".
  - On each card change, focus moves to a region labelled "Card 5 of 17: featured work" and the live region announces the same string, so it is read twice.
  - `document.title` stays "capable" in every lesson.
- **Fix:**
  - Wrap the `//` prefixes in `aria-hidden` spans.
  - In tiles, hide the art from screen readers and point `aria-labelledby` at the title, promise and status.
  - Keep one announcement: either move focus or use the live region, not both.
  - Set the title per lesson ("Own Your Exit · capable").
- **Lane:** app.

**U12. Progress bars are hard to read in yellow and lime, and the empty bar looks like an input.** Shots: `n01-firstrun-390.png` (tile bar), `b-43-counter-320.png` (yellow segments).
- **What's wrong:** an unstarted tile shows an empty outlined box that looks like a text field. Done and todo segments differ only by fill: yellow on cream is 1.29:1, lime 1.43:1, cyan 1.95:1. That fails WCAG 1.4.11 (3:1 for graphical objects).
- **Fix:**
  - Hide the bar until a lesson is started; show "not started · 3 min" instead.
  - Give done segments an ink tick or hatch, and give todo segments a dashed outline.
- **Lane:** app.

**U13. On desktop, home is a narrow column and some phone wording remains.** Shots: `b-50-desktop-home.png`, `d01b-desktop-home-full-small.png`, `d02-desktop-cover.png`, `d04-desktop-sheet.png`.
- **What's wrong:**
  - Home is a single 440px column on a 1280px screen (about 65% empty), about 3,700px tall.

  - At ≥900px, use a two-column tile grid with the "you" panel as a sticky sidebar.

**U14. The empty "you" panel loses the candy colours.** Shot: `b-03-home-you.png`, compare `n11-you-after-1-done.png`.
- **What's wrong:** unearned trait bars are blank cream, so the panel looks disabled and the brand colours vanish. The counts are 11px mono.

**U15. "Resume" appears at 0%, and the End key earns the trait.** Shot: `n10-home-after-1-done.png` (lesson 2 shows RESUME with an empty bar after its cover was only seen).
- **What's wrong:** opening only the cover sets `pos`, so the tile says "Resume" with an empty bar. Pressing End on a cover jumps to the last card and marks the lesson done (verified in `localStorage`).

**U16. Lesson text can't be selected.** No screenshot; verified through computed style.
- **What's wrong:** the deck sets `user-select: none` and `-webkit-touch-callout: none`, and a long press is captured as "hold". Readers can't copy a line, look up a word or translate.

**U17. Practice steps don't look tappable, and finishing them gives no response.** Shots: `b-16-own-your-exit-practice.png`, `a06-practice-ticked.png`.

## Logo and favicon: three directions toward "citizen-like"

The current mark is "capable" in rounded single-colour letters, which reads like a friendly rounded font (`z03-home-logo-zoom.png`). The icon is four candy dots, which reads as a generic toy or "apps" glyph and blurs into a smudge at 16px (`i01-icons.png`). Neither shows a person.

1. **The standing "l".** Replace the "l" in the wordmark with a citizen: a round head on a rounded stem, the same figure as the person in the Own Your Exit scene, optionally holding a small bag. The other letters stay. The favicon and app icon are that figure alone, magenta with an ink outline and hard shadow on cream. At 16px it stays legible as a dot over a bar. Each trait gets the figure in its colour for chips and share cards. This is cheap: it is one new glyph in `GLYPHS` (`art.mjs`).
2. **Four at the table.** Evolve the current icon: give each candy dot shoulders (a half-pill torso) so the 2×2 becomes four people, or set them in a shallow arc like an assembly. The community is literally the four traits. For 32/16px, drop to two figures (cyan and magenta) so they stay readable. The maskable version centres the arc inside the safe zone.
3. **The citizen's stamp.** A passport or visa stamp: a rounded rectangle with an ink rim and a dashed inner border (Beep's dashed rule), holding a lowercase "c" whose opening is a door ajar. That ties together *exit*, *mobility* and *belonging*. The favicon is the c-door in ink on yellow. It doubles as the visual system for earned traits (D1): every lesson stamps your passport.

## Delight: five buildable ideas (calm, bold, no guilt)

- **D1. Passport stamps.** Finishing a lesson presses a trait stamp onto the trait card: an SVG rubber stamp in the trait colour with an ink rim, rotated −6°, showing the lesson number and date. Under reduced motion it fades in. The "you" panel becomes a passport page that collects the stamps; four empty dashed slots show what's left. Data: add `doneAt` to the store.
- **D2. Share your trait.** A "share" button on the trait card renders a 1080×1350 PNG in the browser by drawing the existing SVGs onto a canvas: the trait Shout, the grant line, the lesson scene and the logo. It uses `navigator.share({files})`, with a download as fallback. There is no server and no tracking. Once U16 allows selection, add "share this line" on idea cards, which makes a quote card in the same style.
- **D3. The logo fills as you grow.** Home's logo starts with outlined letters, and they fill in the colours of the traits you've earned (`logoFill()` already exists; home passes `filled: 7` today). A Pixel caption reads "// 5 letters to go". Progress shows at the very top without counters or badges.
- **D4. "Did you try it?" check-in.** Three days after a lesson is finished, its tile wears a small sticker: "// tried it? 2 of 4 steps". Tapping it deep-links straight to the practice card. Ticking every step stamps "done this week" on the tile. It nudges doing rather than reading, never mentions a broken streak, and the sticker disappears on its own after a week.
- **D5. Your one line.** The question card gets an optional one-line field, "write one line, only you see it", stored locally. The trait card echoes it back in Playfair: "you said: '…'". The counter card gets two sticker buttons, "convinced" and "not yet", with the choice remembered and shown on the "you" panel as a quiet tally. It is reflective and private, and makes the deck feel like a conversation.
